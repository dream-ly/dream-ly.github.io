Hi, this is Ying. I am a Human-Computer Interaction and Human-centered AI researcher studying how AI agents enter intimate, domestic, and posthumous human relationships, and how they reshape identity, care, trust, cooperation, and social protocols.

My work examines AI not only as a tool for productivity, but also as a social and relational infrastructure. I am interested in how people live with AI systems in emotionally complex contexts, such as digital legacy and AI afterlife, blended family coordination, long-distance intergenerational communication, migration, aging, divorce, and everyday wellbeing.

I combine system building, interaction design, qualitative inquiry, quantitative analysis, and field deployment to study these questions in real-world settings. Across projects, I aim to turn human insights into deployable socio-technical systems that are technically robust, socially meaningful, emotionally aware, and ethically grounded.

Research focus:

  • AI agents in intimate, domestic, and posthumous life
  • Identity, care, trust, and cooperation in human-AI relationships
  • Family technologies for coordination, distance, and social support
  • Human agency, wellbeing, and appropriate reliance in AI systems

I received interdisciplinary training in technology and design, which grounds my work in HCI and Human-centered AI. I am currently pursuing my Master of Science, advised by Prof. Carman Neustaedter at the School of Interactive Arts and Technology, Simon Fraser University. Previously, I earned my B.Eng. in Computer Science, East China Normal University with the highest honors.
